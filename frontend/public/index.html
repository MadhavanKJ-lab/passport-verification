Passport Verification

Upload a passport image. It is read with OCR, checked by an AI reviewer against the MRZ rules, and analysed for image manipulation (TruFor, B-Free and classical checks). Results are decision support, not an authentication verdict.

1. Upload

Drop an image or click to choose JPG or PNG, up to 15 MB

Your image is sent to the verification workflow and Azure-hosted services. The forensic service keeps nothing after responding; this page stores nothing.

What runs

  1. 1
    OCRPaddleOCR reads every line, including the MRZ.
  2. 2
    AI validationChecks fields, MRZ check digits and visual consistency.
  3. 3
    ForensicsTruFor, B-Free and OpenCV heuristics look for edits and synthetic content.
Results will appear here. A full check usually takes two to four minutes.
Forensic scores are probabilistic. A low score does not prove a document is genuine.
